# Merge rules

A merge joins two spellings into one concept. A person decides each merge by reading the spellings in their contexts. The rules below were fixed before measurement and applied again, unchanged, in the merge audit of 2026-11-06.

## Joined

Forms of the same word in the same language, when the contexts support it.

- inflection, such as singular and plural or gender agreement. *ghirlanda* and *ghirlande*, *martora* and *martore*, *ricamato* and *ricamata*
- old spellings. *ariento* and *argento*, *ghamurre* and *gamurra*, *grillanda* and *ghirlanda*
- OCR misreadings that can be read with confidence in their context. *lattitij* and *lattizzi*
- abbreviated or truncated forms of a word already in the table
- traditional and simplified forms, and known variant characters, of the same Chinese word. *補子* and *补子*, *袴* and *裤*

The concept label is the full form of the word, never a cut stem. Chinese labels are written in simplified characters.

## Not joined

- words formed differently, with a prefix, a suffix, a diminutive or a change of word class. *indorato* and *dorato*, *ricamatura* and *ricamato*
- near synonyms
- a material and the object made of it

## Other decisions in the merge table

- generic. A word that names dress in general, such as *vestimento*, *panni*, 衣服 or 首饰, is marked generic and left out of the measures. Where a word is generic in some passages and specific in others, the decision is made for each occurrence.
- delete. Surfaces that do not name a dress item, such as verbs, adjectives of quality, groups of people or wrong word splits, are deleted.
- category. When the model's category is wrong, the table sets the category for the concept.

## Why this matters

On the 19 demo records, merging moves *lattizzo* from two unrelated entries to one concept that appears outside the law in 1395 and in a law in 1472. It moves *ghirlanda* from outside-first to undecided, because the word is already in the law of 1356. In the full corpus, leaving Florentine spellings unmerged raises the number of concepts from 331 to 482 and changes the count of outside-first against law-first concepts from 14 against 14 to 13 against 9. The merge table is therefore the place to look first when checking results built with this method.
