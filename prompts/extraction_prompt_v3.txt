You will read one historical record about dress. It may be in old
Italian, classical Chinese, or a modern scholarly summary in French or
English.

List every word or short phrase in the record that names one of these:
garment, headwear_jewelry, material_technique, color, pattern, generic.

Rules
1. Copy each term exactly as it appears. Do not translate. Do not
   correct spelling, even when the text has an OCR error.
2. List each distinct term once. List it again only if it has a
   different wearer in another part of the record.
3. Split a material, color or technique word from the object it
   describes. Examples: 金钗 gives 金 and 钗. scaccato in seta gives
   scaccato and seta. cioppa di domaschino bianco gives cioppa,
   domaschino and bianco.
4. Split two object names that stand side by side. Examples: 领袖
   gives 领 and 袖. 袜舄 gives 袜 and 舄. cinture e cingoli gives
   cinture and cingoli.
5. Keep a term whole when:
   - splitting would leave only a general word such as 衣 or 服
     (翟衣, 鞠衣, 团衫);
   - the first part only gives shape, style or size (花钗, 小袖袍,
     唐巾);
   - it is a fixed fabric or technique compound (织金, 销金, 描金,
     panno di lana);
   - it is not clear how to split it.
6. Category by where the thing sits. Pearls, gold, gems or fur that
   form part of a named object are material_technique. The same
   things worn on their own are headwear_jewelry. Trimmings sewn on
   a garment are material_technique. Belts and sashes are garment.
7. Use generic for words that mean clothing or ornament in general,
   such as 衣服, 首饰, vestimento, robba, ornamento.
8. Only list things worn on the body. Do not list verbs of wearing,
   cosmetics, hair or body parts, or colors and materials that do not
   describe dress (for example, in a dye or cosmetic recipe).
9. Include terms that appear in a ban or a negation.
10. If the record names nothing of this kind, return an empty list.
    Do not add anything that is not in the text.
11. For each term, give "wearer": female, male, both, or unclear.
    Use female when the text ties the item to women, girls, wives,
    courtesans or female servants; male when it ties the item only to
    men; both when it names both; unclear otherwise.

Return JSON only, with no other text, in this form:
{"record_id": "...", "terms": [{"surface": "...", "category": "...",
"wearer": "...", "context": "up to 12 characters or 6 words around the term"}]}
