You will read one clause from a sumptuary law about dress. It may be in
Latin, old Italian or classical Chinese, or a modern summary of such a
clause.

Give the clause one code.

item   The clause centres on a thing. It sets the use, number, size or
       value of a garment, ornament, material, technique, color or
       pattern. It applies to everyone, or to everyone except a few
       exempt groups.
rank   The clause centres on a rank or status group. It lists what that
       group may or may not use, so as to set it apart from other groups.
       Signs are a named group together with words such as may use,
       only, must not presume to use, or so as to be distinguished from.
       A clause that gives different standards to different groups is
       also rank.
mixed  Both centres are present and neither is clearly primary. Use it
       rarely.

Decide in this order.
1. Is the clause addressed to a specific rank or status group? If not,
   code item.
2. If it is, does it give that group things that differ from what other
   groups receive? If so, code rank.
3. If it only forbids something to that group, with no contrast between
   groups, code item and name the group in applies_to.
4. If step 2 and step 3 both seem to fit, code mixed.

Examples
- Florence 1356. Women may not show fringes or ermine, squirrel or
  rabbit linings at cuffs and slits. Knights' wives are exempt. -> item
- Florence 1562. Married noble women may wear up to three gold rings, a
  gold chain and a gold belt within set values, in pure gold only.
  -> rank
- Florence 1562. Married women who are not noble may not wear a gold
  frontlet, garland or belt, only pure silver ones. -> rank
- Florence 1546. Prostitutes may not wear silk and must wear a veil with
  a yellow stripe. -> rank
- 洪武三年 士庶妻，首饰用银镀金，耳环用金珠，钏镯用银，服浅色团衫。 -> rank
- 天顺二年 官民衣服不得用蟒龙、飞鱼、斗牛、大鹏、像生狮子、四宝相花、
  大西番莲、大云花样。 -> item
- 成化十年 禁官民妇女不得僭用浑金衣服、宝石首饰。 -> item
- 正德元年 令军民妇女不许用销金衣服帐幔、宝石首饰镯钏，娼妓不许用金首饰、
  银镯钏。 -> rank

"reason": "one sentence of at most 25 words"}
